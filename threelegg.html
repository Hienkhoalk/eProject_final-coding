Three-legged race

DA three-legged race is a race where two people run together with one person's left leg tied to the other person's right leg. The goal is to finish before the other pairs. 

            
How to play

            Use fabric strips or scarves to tie the runners' legs together 
            Have the runners run together as if they have three legs 
            Fun and benefits 
            Three-legged races can be a fun way to stay active
            They can help kids work together and improve their mood
            They can help kids learn to solve problems
            World records 
            The longest distance run three-legged in 24 hours is 72.7 miles, set by Gary Shaughnessy and Andy Tucker in 2021
            The world record for the most pairs in a three-legged race is 768, set by Caritas of Hong Kong in 2019
