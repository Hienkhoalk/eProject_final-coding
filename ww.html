Werewolves

To play Werewolf, players are secretly assigned roles like "Villager" or "Werewolf," and during each round, the game alternates between "night" where Werewolves secretly choose a victim to kill, and "day" where players discuss and vote to eliminate who they suspect is a Werewolf, aiming to eliminate all Werewolves before they outnumber the villagers; the game ends when either all Werewolves are killed or when there are too few villagers left to fight back. 

            Key steps:
            Assign roles:
            A moderator secretly deals roles to each player, usually including Werewolves and Villagers, with potential additional special roles like Seer (can check someone's role) or Doctor (can save someone from being killed at night). 
            Night phase:
            Everyone closes their eyes. 
            The moderator wakes up the Werewolves who silently choose a victim to "kill". 
            If applicable, the moderator wakes up special roles like the Seer or Doctor to perform their actions. 
            Everyone closes their eyes again. 
            Day phase:
            Everyone opens their eyes and the moderator reveals who was "killed" during the night. 
            Players discuss who they think is a Werewolf, trying to gather clues from others' behavior and statements. 
            Players vote on who they believe is a Werewolf and eliminate that player from the game. 
            Important points to remember:
            Secrecy is key: Players must keep their roles secret and try to deceive others. 
            Social deduction: The game relies on observing others' behavior and analyzing their statements to identify Werewolves. 
            Moderator role: The moderator guides the game, ensuring everyone follows the rules and keeps the night actions confidential. 
            
            Variations:
            
            Special roles:
            
            Many variations add more complex roles like the "Cupid" who pairs up two lovers, or the "Tanner" who can only win if killed with a Werewolf. 
            Winning conditions:
            Some variations may require the villagers to identify all Werewolves to win, while others might have different victory conditions depending on the roles present.
